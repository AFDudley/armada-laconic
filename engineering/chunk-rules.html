Rules for the chunk briefs and their execution

Operator rulings, 2026-10-20.

  1. Start on the 5090. Every implementation pebble starts on the 5090 (exophial dispatcher, max_workers: 1). A worker that outgrows the window escalates to Sonnet through contextPromotion; the 5090 then takes the next pebble. At most 4 Sonnet sessions run at once, counting escalated workers and brief writers.
  2. The husband reviews. No separate reviewer agent. The husband of each family reviews every chunk before it merges, and reviews the audit-critical chunks (P1, P2, N1, N2, G3) with extra care. Review findings become child pebbles in the family.
  3. Reuse the armada-nitro e2e work. Acceptance for every chunk, in every repo, uses the armada-nitro environment and gates, not a new harness: - the laconic-so fixturenet and reconciler (armada-nitro docs/ENVIRONMENT.md, briefs E1 to E9 in docs/tasks/); - the gates under armada-nitro/env/gates/: nitro-e2e, pool-e2e, watchtower, hub-custodian-frost. A chunk that changes the pool, the circuits, governance or the SDK extends the existing reconciler and gates (for example a new step in pool-e2e, or a new gate directory beside them) rather than building a separate fixturenet. The gate chunks G7, N7 and C5 are extensions of these gates (N8 was withdrawn on 2026-10-22: there is no protocol swap fee).
  4. Fixturenet first, then Sepolia, then mainnet.
  5. Estimates use ESTIMATION.md in this directory: points, chunks of at most 9 points for the 5090.
  6. The handoff is the live state. armada-laconic/notes/handoff-2026-10-20.md §0 records the state of every family, brief, pebble and gate. The lead updates it and commits it to main whenever any of them changes. Husbands report state changes to the lead; they do not edit the handoff. Every brief names the handoff row it moves (family and chunk id).
